Clause 8.1: Delivery Schedule
Supplier shall deliver materials according to the agreed delivery schedule.

Clause 8.4: Delay and Liquidated Damages
Where delay is attributable to the supplier, contractual delay remedies may apply after the contractual grace period.

Clause 9.2: Material Quantity
Supplier shall provide the quantities stated in the purchase order and approved bill of quantities.

Clause 10.3: Quality Acceptance
Materials failing inspection may be rejected and replaced by the supplier.

Clause 12.1: Notice of Delay
The supplier shall notify the client promptly of circumstances likely to affect delivery.

Clause 14.2: Change Orders
Changes in scope or quantities shall be handled through an approved change order process.
